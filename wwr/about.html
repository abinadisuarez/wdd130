History

What started as a shared passion among a small group of friends has grown into the trusted travel resource you see today. Founded in 2012, our company began with a simple idea: travel should feel personal, transparent, and exciting from the very first click.

Over the years, we've evolved from sharing informal trip notes with a handful of explorers to building a full-service platform that connects you with unique destinations and unforgettable experiences. Through every change, our core mission remains the same.
