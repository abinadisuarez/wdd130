Venezuela

Venezuela, located on the northern coast of South America, is a country of stunning natural contrasts, from the snow-capped peaks of the Andes to the vast plains of Los Llanos and the pristine Caribbean beaches along its coastline. It is home to the world's highest uninterrupted waterfall, Angel Falls, as well as the ancient tepuis (table-top mountains) of Canaima National Park. Rich in oil reserves and biodiversity, Venezuela has a vibrant cultural heritage blending Indigenous, African, and Spanish influences, reflected in its music, dance, and cuisine. Despite facing significant economic and social challenges in recent years, the country's breathtaking landscapes and resilient spirit continue to define its unique identity.
